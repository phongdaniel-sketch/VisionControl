# Unified gesture fine tuning

Use Gestures > Fine tuning as the single location for current gesture thresholds.
Group controls into Click & Drag, Scroll / Zoom / Volume, and Keys & Symbols.
Move the four active pinch controls from Advanced, preserving stored values and
validation. Hide legacy activation frames, drag hold time and scroll deadzone;
also hide legacy confidence tuning (the current feature extractor uses 0.7).
Remove Advanced navigation. Do not change gesture behavior or personal profiles.
