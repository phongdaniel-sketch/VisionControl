# Contact right click and paired-curl middle click

Applies to Palm and current Index fingertip (thumb_lock) modes.

- Left click remains index curl/re-extension.
- Middle click is index AND middle curl/re-extension; middle-only curl does not
  click. Existing curl confirmation and timeout remain unchanged.
- Right click occurs on the first recognized thumb/middle contact, not release
  or a long hold. One click per contact, with hysteresis; both pinches must be
  visibly separated before arming/rearming, including after pause/hand loss.
- Contact freezes the pointer; it must not initiate left drag, scroll, volume,
  zoom or symbol actions. Ambiguous simultaneous thumb/index and thumb/middle
  contact sends no click. An existing left drag retains priority.
- Keep OS release safety, legacy pointer modes and keyboard gestures unchanged.
- Update guide, calibration rehearsal and illustrative hand together. Existing
  personal geometric calibration remains valid; no file migration or overwrite.
