# Aim and click usability

Space toggles pause/resume in the focused Controller window, except during
calibration where it captures a boundary. Ignore auto-repeat and do not resume
while Settings or an error is active. Keep P supported; show shortcut tooltip.
Mouse-only pointing may tolerate moderately bent index (130 degree joint,
positive wrist reach), with no other extended fingers; clear fist still stops.
Do not infer a hidden fingertip or relax keyboard finger-count rules.
Adaptive smoothing adds a small display-scaled resting radius. Freeze cursor
when an eligible pinch approaches the release threshold, through confirmation
and the first drag-hold interval; release anchoring after opening, hand loss,
pause or exceptions. Keep click thresholds and preparation safeguards intact.
